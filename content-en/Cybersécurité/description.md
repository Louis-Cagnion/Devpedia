# Description

This category covers cybersecurity across the board, independently of any specific language or framework: the main families of flaws and who exploits them, the principles that avoid them from the design stage (validation, least privilege, defense in depth), protecting secrets and data (cryptography), the security of the dependency chain and of APIs, methods for testing and auditing a system, the human side (social engineering, phishing), and the OWASP Top 10 as a synthesis reference. Language-specific content (e.g. `filter_input()` in PHP) stays in its own technology chapter and is linked to from here rather than duplicated.
