# Conception à grande échelle

The chapters [Code quality and architecture](/?c=qualite-performance-et-outils&s=qualite-et-architecture-du-code&p=qualite-et-architecture-du-code) and [High-traffic databases](/?c=donnees&s=bases-de-donnees&p=bases-de-donnees-a-fort-trafic) deal respectively with the quality of a given piece of code and the sizing of a given database. This section changes scale: how to reason about an entire application — client, servers, databases, network — when it has to serve millions of simultaneous users, without ever going down. This is the subject of **"system design"**: a way of thinking about architecture at a high level (components and their exchanges, not code), used both in technical interviews and in the real design of large-scale products.
