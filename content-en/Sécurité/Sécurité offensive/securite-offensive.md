# Sécurité offensive

[Cybersecurity](/?c=cybersecurite) covers application and web vulnerabilities, and how to protect against them. This category shifts the angle: understanding how a system is actually attacked at the lowest level (memory, binary, operating system), to build an attacker's eye rather than a defender's. The two complement each other: you protect best what you know how to attack.

> **Legal framework:** everything that follows applies only in an authorized environment (a [CTF](/?c=securite&s=securite-offensive&p=ctf-capture-the-flag)-style challenge, a dedicated lab machine, a system you own, or one for which you have a written mandate). The same reminder already made for [pentesting](/?c=cybersecurite&p=tests-et-audit-de-securite) applies here: without explicit authorization, the exact same action becomes illegal, even with no intent to harm.
