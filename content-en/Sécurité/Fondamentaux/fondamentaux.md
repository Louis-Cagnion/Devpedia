# Fondamentaux

This subject lays the groundwork for authentication: the difference between proving your identity (authentication) and what you're allowed to do once identified (authorization), the different ways to prove who you are, and how to store a password without leaving it readable if the database leaks.

The other subjects in this category (Sessions and Tokens, Delegation and Identity Federation, Strengthening Authentication) all build on these notions: this is the starting point for approaching authentication with no prior background.
