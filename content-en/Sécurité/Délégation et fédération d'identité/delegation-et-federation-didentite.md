# Délégation et fédération d'identité

The previous chapters cover how a site manages its own accounts (passwords, sessions, tokens). This subject looks at a different approach: delegating all or part of that responsibility to a trusted third party, rather than handling everything yourself. A "Log in with Google" button or a centrally managed company account are the two most common examples.
