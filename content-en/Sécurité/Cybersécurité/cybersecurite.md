# Cybersécurité

This category covers cybersecurity across the board, independently of any specific language or framework: [the main families of flaws](/?c=securite&s=cybersecurite&p=types-de-failles) and who exploits them, [the principles that avoid them from the design stage](/?c=securite&s=cybersecurite&p=principes-de-developpement-securise) (validation, least privilege, defense in depth), [protecting secrets](/?c=securite&s=cybersecurite&p=gestion-des-secrets) and data ([cryptography](/?c=securite&s=cybersecurite&p=cryptographie-appliquee)), the security of [the dependency chain](/?c=securite&s=cybersecurite&p=securite-des-dependances) and of [APIs](/?c=securite&s=cybersecurite&p=securite-api-web), [methods for testing and auditing a system](/?c=securite&s=cybersecurite&p=tests-et-audit-de-securite), the human side ([social engineering, phishing](/?c=securite&s=cybersecurite&p=ingenierie-sociale-et-phishing)), and the [OWASP Top 10](/?c=securite&s=cybersecurite&p=owasp-top-10) as a synthesis reference. Language-specific content (e.g. `filter_input()` in [PHP](/?c=langages&s=php&p=php)) stays in its own technology chapter and is linked to from here rather than duplicated.
