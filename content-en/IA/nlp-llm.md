# NLP and LLM

This part covers automated language processing, from classic **NLP** to modern **large language models (LLMs)**: how text becomes usable by a model, how to structure a request (*prompt engineering*), and how to compensate for the limits of an LLM alone (bounded memory, no knowledge of the real world after its training) with external data (RAG) or the ability to act (agents).

Builds directly on the architectures detailed in [Fundamentals of Deep Learning](/?c=ia&p=fondamentaux-du-deep-learning), in particular Transformers.
