# AI Voice

This part covers speech synthesis (*text-to-speech*, text → audio): a third type of generative model, alongside text ([NLP and LLM](/?c=ia&p=nlp-llm)) and image ([Vision and OCR](/?c=ia&p=vision-et-ocr)). From classic concatenative synthesis to modern neural models, through voice cloning and its ethical stakes, evaluating perceived quality, and going to production.

Devpedia itself uses speech synthesis (the site's automatic audio playback, via the browser's Web Speech API, a feature of the site rather than a pedagogical chapter): several chapters refer to it as a concrete example rather than staying purely theoretical.

Builds on the architectures detailed in [Fundamentals of Deep Learning](/?c=ia&p=fondamentaux-du-deep-learning) (CNNs, RNNs, Transformers, attention), applied here to audio rather than text or image.
