# Vision and OCR

This part covers models that process images rather than text: character recognition (**OCR**), from classic pattern recognition to deep learning models, then their variants (layout detection, "Document AI" models, training, evaluation, production deployment).

Builds on the architectures detailed in [Fundamentals of Deep Learning](/?c=ia&p=fondamentaux-du-deep-learning) (CNNs, RNNs, Transformers), applied here to images rather than text. For what happens upstream (extracting the image from a document) and downstream (rebuilding the structure of a recognized table), see the [Document Processing](/?c=traitement-de-documents) section.
