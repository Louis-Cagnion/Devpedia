# Production and Governance

This part covers what surrounds an LLM once it's actually running in production, beyond its internal workings: monitoring a system in use, the obligations that apply to the data passed through it, applicable European regulation, and the infrastructure layers of an end-to-end AI application.

Assumes the notions from [NLP and LLM](/?c=ia&p=nlp-llm) are already acquired: these chapters talk about the LLM in use, not its internal workings.
