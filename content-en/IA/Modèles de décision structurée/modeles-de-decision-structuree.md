# Structured Decision Models

This part covers a family of models distinct from [generative LLMs](/?c=ia&s=nlp-llm&p=nlp-et-llm): rather than producing free text, a **structured decision model** (a category known as "System One") answers closed questions against a given state, with output always in the right format and a calibrated probability. Jev, published by TypeSafe AI, serves as the running example throughout this part.

Builds on the notions of [LLM](/?c=ia&s=nlp-llm&p=nlp-et-llm), [hallucination](/?c=ia&s=nlp-llm&p=llm-en-production), and [function calling/JSON Schema](/?c=ia&s=nlp-llm&p=agents) already covered in NLP and LLM.
